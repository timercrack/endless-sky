	`Energy consumed and heat generated per second when this ship is firing all its weapons. This is in addition to the "idle" amount. Note that weapons may consume or generate more energy or heat than this at the moment they fire, and this is the average value over time.`

	`Energy consumed and heat generated per second when this ship is recharging shields and repairing its hull at the same time. This is in addition to the "idle" amount.`

	`Energy consumed and heat generated per second when this ship is repairing its hull. This is in addition to the "idle" amount.`

tip "ship: charging shields:"
	`Energy consumed and heat generated per second when this ship is recharging its shields. This is in addition to the "idle" amount.`

tip "ship: net change:"
	`The sum of the energy consumed and heat generated per second from the above energy and heat values. Represents the worst case scenario when all possible systems are active at once.`

tip "ship: capacity:"
	`Energy capacity allows a ship to temporarily draw more energy than it produces, and can be increased by installing batteries.`
	`Heat capacity is the highest your heat generation can be without eventually causing your ship to overheat, and is influenced by the mass of the ship. If the "net change" value for heat is greater than this value, then the ship can overheat itself. Weapons with high firing heat per shot can cause you to become overheated even if "net change" is below heat capacity.`

tip "ship: surge:"
	`This ship lacks energy storage. Therefore, this is the greatest amount of energy that can be drawn by any of this ship's systems in a single instant, such as the energy required for a single shot of a weapon. Systems that require more than this amount of energy to activate will be unusable.`

# Gamerules
tip "Depreciative aging"
	`Controls whether age-based depreciation is active. Even if depreciative aging is disabled, captured and plundered goods can still be given separate depreciation values.`

tip "Minimum value"
	`The lowest value that an outfit or ship can depreciate to relative to its full price. All purchased items start at 100% value and then depreciate toward this value according to the other depreciation gamerules.`

tip "Captured value"
	`The value that captured outfits and ships will have. An outfit is considered captured if it is installed on a ship that is captured. Installed outfits taken from the ship before it is captured are instead considered plundered.`
	`If this value is above the "Minimum value", then captured outfits will continue to depreciate toward the minimum value. This value is allowed to be below the minimum value, in which case the items will be locked to that value.`
	`Note that modifying this value in an existing save file will not cause previously captured items to begin aging if this value is moved to above "Minimum value".`

tip "Plundered value"
	`The value that plundered outfits will have. An outfit is considered plundered if it is installed on a ship and removed from that ship during a boarding action.`
	`If this value is above the "Minimum value", then plundered outfits will continue to depreciate toward the minimum value. This value is allowed to be below the minimum value, in which case the outfits will be locked to that value.`
	`Note that modifying this value in an existing save file will not cause previously plundered items to begin aging if this value is moved to above "Minimum value".`

tip "Cargo is plunder"
	`If false, outfits taken from a ship's cargo hold or which are still in the ship's cargo hold at the moment it is captured will not be considered plundered and will use the "Minimum value" to determine their worth.`
	`If true, such outfits will use "Plundered value" instead.`

tip "Grace period"
	`新购装备或飞船开始折旧前的天数。这让你可以短暂试用，若不满意可原价售回。`

tip "Maximum age"
	`宽限期结束后，装备或飞船完全折旧所需的天数。`

tip "Daily depreciation"
	`物品每日保留的可折旧价值百分比。仅影响折旧计算中的指数项。若设为100%，物品仍会线性贬值。`

tip "Spawn attempt period"
	`游戏尝试生成人物飞船的平均帧数。每帧在0到该值-1间随机，若为0则尝试生成。值越高，生成率越低。`

tip "No spawn weight"
	`人物飞船刷出失败权重。刷出尝试失败的概率 = 此值 / (所有人物飞船权重之和 + 此值)。`

tip "NPC max mining time"
	`限制随机生成的采矿型NPC在当前星系中的采矿帧数，避免采空小行星。任务生成船只不受此限。`

tip "Universal frugal threshold"
	`具有「节俭」性格的飞船，仅当其生命值（护盾+船体-失效时的船体值）等于或低于此百分比，或当星区内敌对势力远强于它们时，才会使用消耗弹药或燃料的武器。`

tip "Ships lock when captured"
	`Determines whether a ship's computers are "locked" when captured, disabling some of their abilities. Ships can have their locks removed by rewiring them at a shipyard.`
	`Locked ship restrictions include but are not limited to being unable to be your flagship, fire weapons, use shield generators or hull repair, use fighter or drone bays, and use their cargo space or bunks for missions or trading.`

tip "Rewiring cost multiplier"
	`How much rewiring a ship will cost, relative to the ship's chassis price without depreciation.`

tip "Locked hull value multiplier"
	`How much the hull of a ship will sell for if its computer systems are locked. This stacks multiplicatively with depreciation.`

tip "Universal ramscoop"
	`决定是否在所有飞船上激活“通用冲压集气器”。它能为每艘飞船提供极少量燃料，无论是否安装了常规集气器。其燃料收集效率随距离恒星衰减更严重，必须尽可能靠近恒星才能获得真正增益。启用后可避免在燃料耗尽且星系内没有可求助NPC时被迫重读存档。`

tip "Minimum arrival distance"
	`所有系统的最小抵达距离。适用于超光速驱动和跳跃驱动。若系统定义的距离大于此值，则使用系统定义值。若此游戏规则为“未设置”，则始终使用系统定义值。`

tip "Minimum departure distance"
	`所有系统的最小出发距离。飞船必须距离星系中心超过此距离才能跳跃。若某星系定义的距离更大，则使用该星系的值。`

tip "Roll chance"
	`Controls the chance of a raid fleet spawn attempt occurring each time you jump into a system.`
	`Raid fleets are hostile fleets that can spawn in some systems if your fleet's cargo attractiveness is much higher than its deterrence. (That is, if you have a lot of cargo space and not a lot of firepower to defend it.) If this rule isn't 0%, your fleet's raid fleet spawn chance can be viewed from the player info panel.`

tip "Attraction multiplier"
	`Multiplies the raid fleet attraction value of all the ships in your fleet. 200% means that you would need half as much cargo space to have the same chance to spawn raid fleets, while 50% means you need twice as much.`

tip "Deterrence multiplier"
	`Multiplies the raid fleet deterrence value of all the ships in your fleet. 200% means that you would need half as much firepower to have the same chance to spawn raid fleets, while 50% means you need twice as much.`

tip "Fleet spawn attempts"
	`Each time you jump into a system when your fleet is attractive to raid fleets and the raid fleet roll chance was successful, this is the maximum number of fleets that could possibly spawn`

tip "Permadeath"
	`Whether permadeath mode is active, and how it should behave.`
	`- "lock on death": When you die, this pilot will be locked. You will be unable to load any of its saves after it enters this state, but you'll still have the save files to share and for keeping memories.`
	`- "delete on death": When you die, this pilot's save files will be deleted.`
	`- "lock on takeoff": When you depart from a planet, this pilot will be locked. It will be unlocked again upon landing on a planet. Forces the "Restricted save loading" gamerule to be on.`
	`- "delete on takeoff": When you depart from a planet, this pilot's save file will be deleted and will only be recreated when you land. Forces the "Single save file" and "Restricted save loading" gamerules to be on.`

tip "Single save file"
	`If true, this pilot will only be able to have a single save file at a time. You will be unable to create snapshots and the game will not create autosaves for previous days. Only your most recent landing point will be saved.`

tip "Restricted save loading"
	`If true, this pilot will be unable to reload save files while not on the safety of a planet.`

tip "Profit sharing"
	`Controls whether and how the value of captured ships is shared among the crew members of your flagship.`
	`- "off": You keep the full value of the captured ship.`
	`- "dead crew": Death benefits must be paid to the families of crew members who died in the process of capturing a ship. A minimum payment per casualty may also be applied regardless of a successful capture.`
	`- "surviving crew": Surviving crew members must be paid a bonus based on the value of the captured ship.`
	`- "all crew": Both dead and surviving crew must be paid according to the rules stated above.`

tip "Captain shares"
	`If capture profit sharing is active and you successfully capture a ship, this is the number of shares in the value of the captured ship that you as the captain receive.`

tip "Dead crew shares"
	`If capture profit sharing is set to "dead crew" or "all crew", this is the number of shares in the value of the captured ship that each family of the dead crew members receives.`

tip "Surviving crew shares"
	`If capture profit sharing is set to "surviving crew" or "all crew", this is the number of shares in the value of the captured ship that each surviving crew member receives.`

tip "Minimum per casualty"
	`If death benefits are active and you lose crew during a boarding action, this is the minimum number of credits that must be paid to the families of the crew members who died. This amount must be paid even if you didn't successfully capture a ship.`

tip "Capture value basis"
	`Controls what percentage of the value of a captured ship is used to determine the size of a share. If unset, then a ship is valued at its sale price as it is captured.`

tip "Same government probability"
	`The probability that a projectile will collide with a ship with the same government. The slower moving a projectile is relative to a ship, the less likely it is to cause friendly fire. Weapons with the "safe" attribute cannot cause friendly fire.`

tip "Allied government probability"
	`The probability that a projectile will collide with a friendly ship of the same government. Ships hit this way will not be provoked.`

tip "Fighter probability multiplier"
	`Projectiles that are fired by or intersect with fighters and drones have their chance to cause friendly fire multiplied by this value.`

tip "Carrier exception"
	`Makes carrier groups ignore friendly fire entirely. Carriers will be unable to hit their fighters, and their fighters will not be able to hit each other and their carrier.`

tip "Damage multiplier"
	`This multiplier is applied to damage dealt by friendly fire. Blast damage and damage against ships being actively targeted are not affected.`
